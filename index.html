One book, hedged on both sides

Spot is bought, the same notional is shorted on perps. Price moves cancel out. What's left is the funding rate — and that is what the vault earns.

Neutral by construction

Every long is matched by a short of the same notional. Net delta is held at zero, not managed by hand.

Funded by carry

Income comes from funding paid by leveraged traders. It accrues hourly and does not depend on a rally.

Non-custodial

Vault shares sit in your wallet. Withdrawals are not gated by a request queue or an approval desk.

Deposit

USDC goes into the vault contract on Base. You receive shares at the current NAV.

Hedge

The router opens spot and the offsetting perp short within the same block.

Accrue

Funding lands every hour. NAV rises; shares are redeemable at any time.

Questions

What happens if the market crashes?

The long side loses and the short side gains by the same amount, so the vault NAV barely moves. The risk that matters is funding flipping negative — see the next answer.

What if funding turns negative?

The vault receives funding only when it is positive. When the rate flips, the position is unwound and capital sits in USDC instead of paying to hold the hedge.

How is the price of a share calculated?

NAV per share is total assets under management divided by shares outstanding, updated on-chain every hour after funding settles.

Is there a lock-up?

No. Redemptions are processed in the same block, subject to the venue's withdrawal limits on the underlying exchange.
